Only four business data sources changed: `comparison-data.ts`, `matrix-data.ts`, `china-region-data.ts`, and the curated `trend` field in `mini-data.ts`. Runtime renderers, CSS, chart parameters, public runtime, dependencies and Git operations remain outside this work.

- Category data: six business fixtures use three bounded snapshots. Semester participation varies by actual semester; college course counts retain their planned denominators; covered and uncovered knowledge points still sum to the original per-layer totals. Published-version counts, satisfaction scores, and both period-series values retain their revision-zero examples. Updates vary category relationships rather than translating every value equally. Period slot IDs, original dates across the year boundary, and the previous period's missing fifth day remain unchanged.
- Matrix data: all five families use three bounded snapshots. Weekday/hour activity distinguishes low overnight counts from study peaks. Semester placements remain valid integers on the unchanged 1–8 scale. Similarity preserves shared axes, directed readings, and missing diagonal positions, while available self-similarity is 1. Quantity bubbles retain the fixed area/color domain and the normal zero sample; the category-6/college-6 value 90 remains a large-bubble readout-fit stress sample. Support values remain only the declared three grades. All axis IDs, matrix sizes, encodings, steps, source IDs and special-cell kinds remain intact.
- China data: only numeric Mock statistics changed. Three varied snapshots replace indefinite growth by listing order. Each valid reading remains within its initial fifty-person bucket, so updates do not force same-layer scale expansion. The 399 licensed identities, geometry assets, loaders and hierarchy are untouched. Normal zero/null observations, all-zero, all-missing, invalid and unmatched-record cases remain distinct.
- Mini data: only normal/long/single eight-day activity profiles changed. Broad rises and falls are derived from the existing cohort count, with daily values between zero and that count. Three activity profiles combine with the original four-step quantity cycle for a complete twelve-step cycle. The already approved IDs, labels, counts, composition parts, completed values and goals are byte-equivalent in 84 protected-field snapshots covering twelve revisions and seven states. The original zero/missing/invalid trend fixtures remain intact.

`network-data.ts` remains byte unchanged. Its four business graphs already demonstrate categories, directed relations, multiple relation types, cross-category and bidirectional connections, feedback cycles, and bounded three-step weight/position updates. Altering them would provide little additional data-expression value while risking established topology/layout evidence.
